Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who this applies to

YOUR-APP-NAME is a private tool operated by one person for their own use. The only person whose TikTok account is connected to it is its owner. It has no other users and no public sign-ups.

What information it handles

When the owner connects TikTok, the tool receives an access token and a refresh token from TikTok, along with the account's TikTok open ID. These let the tool upload videos to the owner's drafts. The tool also handles the videos it creates and their captions.

Where it is stored

The tokens are kept in the private settings of a Google Apps Script project controlled by the owner. Videos are created in a private GitHub workflow run and are sent to TikTok. The tool does not keep a separate database of personal information.

How it is used

The information is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok drafts. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Sharing

The information is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone, except that videos are sent to TikTok, and the tool runs on Google and GitHub services, which have their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the connection is active. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from TikTok's app permissions settings, and can delete the stored tokens from the Apps Script project.
